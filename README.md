### About the Experiment 
The goal of this experiment is to understand how people search for things in a group. It focuses on two types of searches:

- **Singleton Search:** Finding something that stands out because of one unique feature, like color or shape.
- **Conjunctive Search:** Finding something that stands out because of a combination of features, like both color and shape.
