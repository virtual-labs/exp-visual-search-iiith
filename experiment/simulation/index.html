Instructions for performing Visual Search Task
  • There will be Upright and Inverted T's of Orange and Blue color displayed on the screen.
  • Your task is to find the Upright Orange T as quickly as possible.
  • There are two types of search tasks:
    • Singleton Search: The target differs by one feature (e.g., color).
    • Conjunctive Search: The target differs by two features (e.g., color and orientation).
  • Press the spacebar as fast as possible when you spot the Upright Orange T within 5 seconds.(You have 5 seconds to respond).
  • Don't press the spacebar for 5 seconds if the target is not found. The trial will end automatically.
  • The number of objects (set size) will vary across trials.
  • Your response time and accuracy will be recorded.
  • At the end of the task, you will see the average search times for both Singleton and Conjunctive Search.
