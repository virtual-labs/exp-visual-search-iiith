## Aim of the Experiment

The goal of this experiment is to understand how people search for things in a group. It focuses on two types of searches:  

1. **Singleton Search**: Finding something that stands out because of one unique feature, like color or shape.  
2. **Conjunctive Search**: Finding something that stands out because of a combination of features, like both color and shape.  

Participants will use a digital tool to perform these tasks. The experiment will measure how accurate they are and how fast they can find the target in different situations. This helps us learn more about how attention and visual searching work.
